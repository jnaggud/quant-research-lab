# Historical research records

These files document experiments from the original research workspace. They include accepted and rejected variants, parameter-search summaries, parity comparisons, and stress studies. Their dates and assumptions matter: “active,” “current,” and “production” refer to the local workflow at the time of writing.

Raw market bars and complete TradingView report/order exports are not distributed. References to those inputs remain for provenance; consult [data notes](../docs/data.md) before attempting reproduction. Workstation paths have been made relative. The public demo's synthetic results are unrelated to these historical figures.

Start with the selected research links in the main README. Treat the remainder as an experiment archive rather than a list of recommended trading strategies.
